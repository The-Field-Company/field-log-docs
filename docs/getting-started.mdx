# Getting Started

FieldLog helps research teams design observation forms, deploy them to phones in
the field, record sightings with photos and GPS—even offline—and export clean
datasets when the survey is done.

## How FieldLog works

1. **Create a session** — Pick a form style (standard fields, multi-page survey,
   or rapid wildlife tally) and configure the questions you need.
2. **Share with your team** — Send a direct link, print a QR code, or invite
   specific team members to a private session.
3. **Collect in the field** — Open the form on any phone or laptop to record
   observations, attach camera-trap or field photos, and capture GPS coordinates
   automatically.
4. **Review and export** — Track live counts, view sightings on an interactive
   map, inspect photos, and download everything as a `.zip` archive.

## Sign in to your account

Once your account is set up (see [Request access](./request-access)), sign in to
open your workspace:

1. Go to [fieldlog.thefieldco.com/login](https://fieldlog.thefieldco.com/login).
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

![The FieldLog sign-in screen](/img/sign-in.png)

FieldLog keeps your session signed in automatically in the browser. Click
**Logout** in the top-right corner whenever you want to sign out on a shared
device.

## Your workspace

After signing in, you land on the **Sessions** list:

![The Sessions list showing active field surveys and submission counts](/img/sessions-list.png)

From the top navigation bar, you can reach everything in a single click:

- **New Session** *(Managers only)* — Launch the 4-step wizard to build a new
  survey or tally.
- **Sessions** — Browse all public sessions and any private sessions you belong
  to, along with live submission totals.
- **Subscriptions** — Jump straight into the forms you actively contribute to.
- **Logout** — Sign out of your account.

## User roles

What you can see and do in FieldLog depends on your role:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Manager** | Create and edit sessions, switch sessions between **Active** and **Inactive** or **Public** and **Private**, manage members, generate QR/invite links, collect data, and export `.zip` archives. |
| **Field Worker** *(Submitter)* | Browse public sessions, join private sessions via invite or direct assignment, subscribe to sessions, and submit observations (online or offline). |
| **Anonymous** *(No account)* | Open any active **Public** session link or QR code and submit observations without signing in. |
