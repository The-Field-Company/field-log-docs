# Getting Started

Welcome to the FieldLog documentation.

These guides walk you through setting up your first session, collecting
observations in the field, and getting your data out when the work is done.

## Create a session

Build your form, drag fields onto the canvas, and publish it.

## Deploy to the field

Share a link or QR code so your team can open the form on any phone.

## Collect data

Submissions arrive with GPS, photos, and timestamps.
