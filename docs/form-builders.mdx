# Form Builders

FieldLog includes three purpose-built form builders so you can match the tool to
your field protocol—from simple camera-trap logs to rapid aerial wildlife counts
and multi-page ecological surveys.

## Choosing the right builder

| Builder | Best for | Key capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic (FormKit)** | Everyday field logs, transect sheets, camera-trap records | Fast question list, 16 input types, required validation, **File Upload** for field photos |
| **Tally Counter** | Rapid species counts, point counts, aerial or roadside tallies | One-tap `+1` buttons, nested category groups, **6,400+ built-in wildlife icons** |
| **Advanced (SurveyJS)** | Detailed multi-page protocols and structured questionnaires | Full-screen drag-and-drop visual studio, multi-page layouts, boolean switches, live preview tab |

---

## 1. Basic (FormKit)

**Basic (FormKit)** is the fastest way to put together a clean, mobile-friendly
observation form.

![Building a form in Basic (FormKit) mode with text, dropdown choices, and file upload fields](/img/builder-formkit.png)

### Add and configure questions

1. Click **+ Add question** at the bottom of the field list.
2. Type the field prompt under **Question** (for example, `Species Name`).
3. Choose an input format under **Type**:
   - **Text**: `Text`, `Text Area`, `Email`, `URL`, `Phone`, `Password`
   - **Number**: `Number`, `Slider` *(lets you configure **Min**, **Max**, and
     **Step**)*
   - **Date & Time**: `Date`, `Time`, `Date & Time`
   - **Choice**: `Dropdown`, `Radio Buttons`, `Checkbox`
   - **Other**: `Color Picker`, `File Upload` *(for attaching field or
     camera-trap photos)*
4. Check **Required** if field workers must answer the question before
   submitting.
5. For **Dropdown** or **Radio Buttons**, click **+ Add choice** to list each
   option, or click `×` beside an option to remove it.
6. To delete a question, click the `×` button in the top-right corner of that
   question's row.

## 2. Tally Counter

**Tally Counter** replaces typing with large, touch-friendly tiles. Every tap in
the field records a timestamped `+1` observation immediately—ideal when counting
animals on the move or working in rain and gloves.

### Organize items and nested groups

You can place species tiles directly on the top level or organize them inside
nested category folders (for example, `Mammals → Hoofed → Buffalo`):

1. Click **+ Add Group** (or **+ Group** inside an existing group) to create a
   category folder such as `Mammals` or `Waterfowl`.
2. Click **+ Add Item** (or **+ Add** inside a group) to add a countable tile
   such as `Buffalo`, `Elephant`, or `Zebra`.
3. Hover over any tile and click `×` in its corner to remove it, or click the
   tile itself to edit its label and icon.

![The Tally Counter builder showing nested Mammals, Hoofed, and Carnivores groups with wildlife silhouettes](/img/builder-tally-grid.png)

### Assign wildlife icons or custom images

Clicking any item or group opens the **Edit Tally Item** dialog:

1. Enter a clear **Label** (for example, `Buffalo`).
2. Click the green **pencil button** on the circular preview to choose an image:
   - **Wildlife icon** — Browse or search FieldLog's built-in library.
   - **Image URL** — Paste any `https://` image link for custom reference photos.
   - **Remove image** — Revert to a clean text-only tile.
3. Click **Save**.

![The Edit Tally Item dialog showing the pencil menu with Wildlife icon, Image URL, and Remove image options](/img/builder-tally-item-editor.png)

### Search 6,400+ wildlife icons

Selecting **Wildlife icon** opens the **Choose a Wildlife Icon** picker, powered
by CyberTracker and aerial wildlife survey silhouettes:

1. Type an animal, track, sign, or group name in the search box (such as
   `elephant`, `heron`, or `tracks`).
2. Click any silhouette card to assign it to your tally tile.

![The Wildlife Icon Picker showing search results for elephant silhouettes and tracks](/img/builder-wildlife-icons.png)

## 3. Advanced (SurveyJS)

Choose **Advanced (SurveyJS)** when your protocol needs multiple pages,
rich question types, or visual drag-and-drop layout control.

1. In Step 3 of the wizard (or on the **Edit Session** page), click **Open
   Visual Editor**.
2. Drag question types from the left toolbox onto your page—including text
   inputs, dropdowns, checkboxes, rating scales, boolean Yes/No switches, and
   more.
3. Click any question on the canvas to configure its title, choices, validation,
   and layout options in the right-hand properties panel.
4. Switch to the **Preview** tab in the top-left corner at any time to test the
   questionnaire interactively.
5. Click **Done** in the top-right corner to return to FieldLog—your changes are
   saved into the session automatically.

![The full-screen SurveyJS visual form builder with toolbox, page canvas, and properties panel](/img/builder-surveyjs.png)
