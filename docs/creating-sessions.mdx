# Creating & Managing Sessions

A **Session** in FieldLog represents a single data-collection project—such as a
weekly bird transect, a night amphibian census, or a camera-trap log. Managers
create sessions in four quick steps and control every aspect of the survey from
the session dashboard.

## Create a new session

Click **New Session** in the top navigation bar to open the 4-step session
wizard.

### Step 1: Details

Give your session a clear title, describe the protocol for your field team, and
choose whether to record GPS coordinates:

1. Enter a **Session Name** (required), such as `Wetland Bird Transect`.
2. Add an optional **Description** explaining what or where to sample.
3. Check **Track GPS location with submissions** if you want FieldLog to capture
   device coordinates with every observation and plot them on a map.
4. Click **Next**.

![Step 1 of the New Session wizard with name, description, and GPS tracking enabled](/img/wizard-step-1-details.png)

### Step 2: Form Type

Pick the form builder that matches how your team records data in the field, then
click **Next**:

- **Basic (FormKit)** — Clean, fast list of standard inputs (text, numbers,
  dropdowns, dates, checkboxes, sliders, and photo uploads).
- **Advanced (SurveyJS)** — Full visual drag-and-drop survey studio for complex
  or multi-page questionnaires.
- **Tally Counter** — Tap-to-count species grid with nested category folders and
  built-in wildlife icons.

![Step 2 of the New Session wizard showing the three form type options](/img/wizard-step-2-form-type.png)

### Step 3: Builder

Add and configure the questions or tally items for your form, then click
**Next**. See the dedicated [Form Builders](./form-builders) guide for a full
walkthrough of all three builders.

### Step 4: Confirm

Review your session settings—**Name**, **Description**, **GPS Tracking**,
**Form Type**, and total **Fields** count. Need to adjust anything? Click
**Back** to return to an earlier step without losing your work.

When everything looks right, click **Create Session**.

![Step 4 of the New Session wizard reviewing the session summary before creation](/img/wizard-step-4-confirm.png)

:::note Default visibility
New sessions start **Active** (open for submissions) and **Private** (visible
only to managers and invited members) so you can test your form before opening
it to a wider audience.
:::

## Tour the session dashboard

Click any session in your **Sessions** list to open its dashboard:

![The Session Dashboard showing live metrics, Form Settings toggles, members, and recent submissions](/img/session-dashboard-overview.png)

At the top of the dashboard, two summary cards give you instant visibility and
control:

- **Overview card (left)**:
  - **Submissions** — Total observations recorded so far.
  - **Contributors** — Number of unique people who have submitted data.
  - **Last Activity** — Relative time since the latest entry (for example,
    `Just now` or `19h ago`).
  - **Share** — Generate a shareable link and QR code (see
    [Sharing, Invites & Subscriptions](./sharing-and-access)).
  - **Add Submission** — Open the live form to record a new entry yourself.
- **Form Settings card (right)** *(Managers only)*:
  - **Preview** and **Edit** buttons in the header.
  - **Active** and **Public** toggle switches.
  - **Members** list (shown when the session is private).

## Preview your form

Want to verify how your form looks before sending a team into the field? Click
**Preview** in the **Form Settings** card.

![Form Preview mode showing how field workers see the form](/img/form-preview.png)

Preview mode renders the exact layout field workers see—with a **Preview**
banner at the top and the submit bar hidden so you don't record test data by
accident. Click **Back to session** to return to the dashboard.

## Edit an existing session

To update a session's title, description, GPS setting, or form questions:

1. Open the session dashboard and click **Edit** in the **Form Settings** card.
2. Modify any details or add, edit, and remove questions on a single page.
3. Click **Update Session** in the bottom-right footer.

![The Edit Session page showing session metadata and form fields in a single view](/img/edit-session.png)

## Open, pause, or change visibility

Managers can change session access at any time using the switches under
**Form Settings**:

- **Active** — Turn **ON** to accept new submissions, or switch **OFF** when a
  field season ends to freeze the dataset and prevent further entries.
- **Public** — Turn **ON** so anyone with the link can view and submit (even
  without an account), or keep **OFF** to restrict the session to invited team
  members.
