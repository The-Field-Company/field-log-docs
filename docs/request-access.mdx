# Request access

FieldLog is available by invitation. To get access, submit a request and a
Field Company agent will set up your environment for you.

## Submit a request

1. Go to [fieldlog.thefieldco.com](https://fieldlog.thefieldco.com).
2. Click **Request Access**.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name** — your full name.
   - **Email** — where we will reach you.
   - **Occupation** — for example, Field Researcher, Biologist, or Student.
4. Complete the security check.
5. Click **Request Access**.

![The request access form](/img/request-access.png)

You should see the confirmation: *"Thanks for your interest! We'll be in touch
soon."*

## What happens next

A Field Company agent reviews your request and contacts you to set up your
environment. This covers your account, sign-in, and anything else you need
before you can start collecting data.

Your details are used only to review your request. See the
[Privacy Policy](https://fieldlog.thefieldco.com/privacy).

Once your account is ready, continue to [Getting Started](./getting-started) to
sign in and explore your workspace.
