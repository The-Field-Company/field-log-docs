# Reviewing & Exporting Data

As observations arrive from the field, the **Session Dashboard** organizes GPS
locations, photos, species counts, and raw records in one place—and lets
managers export everything as a portable `.zip` archive.

## Explore sightings on the interactive map

Whenever a session has **Track GPS location with submissions** enabled, a
**Submission Locations** card appears on the dashboard showing how many records
include coordinates (for example, `5 of 5 with GPS`):

![The Session Dashboard showing the interactive OpenStreetMap GPS pin map above recent photo submissions](/img/dashboard-map.png)

- **Zoom and pan** using the `+` / `−` controls or touch gestures to inspect
  transects, plots, or camera stations on OpenStreetMap.
- **Click any blue pin** to open a popup with the **contributor's username** and
  exact **submission timestamp**.

---

## Browse recent submissions and field photos

On **Basic (FormKit)** and **Advanced (SurveyJS)** sessions, the **Recent
Submissions** table displays the latest observations with columns matching each
question in your form.

When submissions include uploaded photos, thumbnail previews appear right inside
the table. Click any thumbnail to open the full-resolution **Photo Viewer**:

![The full-screen photo viewer lightbox displaying a camera-trap lion photo, file size, and Open original link](/img/dashboard-photo-viewer.png)

From the viewer you can check the file name and size (for example, `84 KB`),
click **Open original** to open the full image in a new browser tab, or press
`Esc` / `✕` to close.

---

## Review Tally Counter totals and activity

For **Tally Counter** sessions, the dashboard replaces the table with **Tally
Results**:

![Tally Results showing aggregated species counts organized by Mammals and Birds groups above the live activity feed](/img/dashboard-tally-results.png)

- **Aggregated summary grid (top)** — Totals every tap per species, neatly
  organized under your nested category groups (such as `Mammals → Hoofed` and
  `Birds → Waterfowl`).
- **Chronological activity feed (bottom)** — Lists every individual tap with who
  recorded it, the species and group path, and when it came in.

---

## Inspect raw submission data (JSON)

Need to check exact GPS coordinates, accuracy in meters, or raw field values for
a specific sighting? Click **any row** in the **Recent Submissions** table (or
any line in the **Tally Results** activity feed) to open the **Submission**
inspector:

![The Submission inspector modal showing contributor, full timestamp, embedded photo preview, and raw JSON fields](/img/dashboard-raw-submission.png)

The inspector shows:

- **Contributor** — Username of the field worker (or `Anonymous` on public
  forms).
- **Submitted** — Full date and time of the observation.
- **Structured payload** — Every recorded key-value field, inline image
  previews for photo attachments, and `_location` (`latitude`, `longitude`, and
  `accuracy`) when GPS tracking is active.

---

## Export your complete dataset (.zip)

When you're ready to analyze your results in R, Python, Excel, or GIS software—or
archive a completed field season—managers can export the entire session in one
click:

1. Open the session dashboard.
2. Click **Export Data** in the top-right corner of **Recent Submissions** (or
   **Tally Results**).
3. FieldLog packages and downloads a `.zip` file named
   `field-log-<session-name>-export-<YYYY-MM-DD>.zip`.

Unzipping the archive gives you a clean, self-contained folder structure:

```text
field-log-trail-camera-log-export-2026-11-12/
├── submissions.json   # All observations, timestamps, contributors & GPS coords
└── images/            # Every confirmed field photo attached to those records
```

Because `submissions.json` uses standard UTF-8 JSON and `images/` stores the
original verified image files, your data is never locked in and drops straight
into any analysis pipeline.
