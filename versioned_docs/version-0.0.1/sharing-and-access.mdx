# Sharing, Invites & Subscriptions

Once your session is ready, you can distribute it to field workers—or open it to
citizen scientists—using QR codes, expiring invite links, direct member
assignment, and subscriptions.

## Public vs. Private sessions

Managers control who can access a session with the **Public** switch under
**Form Settings** on the session dashboard:

| Setting | Who can view & submit | How to share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** (`ON`) | Anyone with the link—including people **without** a FieldLog account (recorded as `Anonymous`). Appears in **Sessions** for all signed-in users. | Direct session link & QR code |
| **Private** (`OFF`) | Only Managers and authorized session **Members**. Hidden from everyone else. | 7-day expiring invite link/QR code, or adding members directly |

---

## Share a public session via QR code or link

For public surveys, volunteers and field technicians can scan a QR code on a
clipboard, trailhead sign, or briefing slide and start submitting right away:

1. Open the session dashboard and make sure **Public** is toggled **ON**.
2. Click **Share** on the left overview card.
3. Show the **QR code** for phones to scan, or click **Copy Link** to paste the
   URL into a message or email.

![The Share Session modal for a public session showing a QR code and direct session URL](/img/share-public-qr.png)

## Invite people to a private session

When **Public** is toggled **OFF**, clicking **Share** automatically generates a
secure **7-day invite token** instead of a public URL:

1. On your private session dashboard, click **Share**.
2. Scan the QR code or click **Copy Link** to share the `/invite/<token>` URL
   with your team.

![The Share Session modal for a private session showing a 7-day expiring invite link and QR code](/img/share-private-invite-qr.png)

### How field workers accept an invite

When a teammate opens your invite link (or scans the QR code):

1. They see the session name, description, and invite expiration date.
2. If they aren't signed in yet, FieldLog prompts them to **Log in** first and
   returns them right back to the invite.
3. Clicking **Join Session** adds them as a session member and opens the session
   dashboard immediately.

![The session invitation screen with session details, expiration date, and Join Session button](/img/invite-accept.png)

:::tip Expired links
Invite links expire automatically after **7 days**. If a link expires before
everyone joins, just click **Share** again on the dashboard to generate a fresh
7-day invite.
:::

## Add or remove private members directly

If your teammates already have FieldLog accounts, you can add or revoke their
access directly on a private session's dashboard—no invite link required:

1. Ensure **Public** is switched **OFF** so the **Members** box appears under
   **Form Settings**.
2. Start typing a teammate's username or email address into **Add members…**.
3. Click their name in the dropdown list to grant access immediately. Each
   member appears as a colored pill.
4. To remove someone from a private session, click the `×` on their name pill.
   Their access is revoked right away.

![Adding team members to a private session using the autocomplete search box](/img/private-members.png)

## Keep track of active work with Subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** page gives field workers a focused launchpad for the
surveys they actively participate in—without hunting through long session lists.

- **Private sessions** you join (via invite or manager assignment) appear in
  **Subscriptions** automatically.
- **Public sessions** have a **Subscribe** button under **Form Settings** on the
  session dashboard so you can pin them to your list too.

Click **Subscriptions** in the top navigation bar to view all your subscribed
sessions:

![The Subscriptions page showing subscribed sessions with Open Form and Unsubscribe actions](/img/subscriptions-page.png)

- Click **Open Form** to jump straight into data entry in the field.
- Click the **session title** to view its dashboard and recent submissions.
- Click **Unsubscribe** when you're done participating in a survey to remove it
  from your list.
