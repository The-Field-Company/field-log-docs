# Collecting Data in the Field

FieldLog forms work in any mobile or desktop browser—and keep working when your
signal drops miles from the nearest cell tower.

## Open a form to record observations

You can reach a session's submission form in several ways:

- From **Subscriptions**, click **Open Form** beside the session.
- From a **Session Dashboard**, click **Add Submission**.
- Scan a **QR code** or open a shared `/sessions/<id>/submit` link directly on
  your phone.

---

## Fill out Basic and SurveyJS forms

### Basic (FormKit) forms

Enter your observation details into each field and tap **Submit** in the bottom
footer bar:

![Filling out a Bird Survey observation form with species, habitat, count, and field notes](/img/collect-standard-form.png)

- If you are **signed in**, saving returns you to the session dashboard with a
  `Submission saved` confirmation.
- If you are submitting **anonymously** on a public form, the form clears itself
  automatically so you can immediately enter the next sighting.

### Advanced (SurveyJS) surveys

Multi-question and multi-page SurveyJS forms display each prompt in clean cards
with built-in page navigation. Answer the questions and tap **Complete** at the
bottom of the final page to record your response:

![Filling out an Amphibian Census form built with SurveyJS](/img/collect-surveyjs.png)

## Count wildlife rapidly with Tally Counter

Tally sessions are designed for fast, heads-up counting without typing or page
reloads:

1. If your session uses category folders (marked with a `▾` arrow), tap a folder
   such as **Mammals** and then **Hoofed** to drill down.
2. Use the breadcrumb trail at the top (`All / Mammals / Hoofed`) to jump back
   up to any parent folder or top-level view in one tap.
3. Tap any species tile—such as **Buffalo** or **Elephant**—each time you spot
   one.

![A Tally Counter form drilled into All / Mammals / Hoofed showing tap-to-count wildlife cards](/img/collect-tally-groups.png)

Every tap flashes `+1` on the card, saves an individual timestamped observation
(with GPS coordinates if enabled), shows a brief `+1 Buffalo` confirmation
toast, and leaves you right on the grid ready for your next sighting.

## Attach photos and capture GPS coordinates

When a session includes a **File Upload** field or has **Track GPS location with
submissions** enabled, FieldLog handles media and coordinates automatically:

![Recording a camera-trap observation with photo upload and the green Location captured badge](/img/collect-photo-gps.png)

### Attaching field or camera-trap photos

1. Tap the **Photo** (file upload) field.
2. On a phone or tablet, take a photo directly with your camera or pick an
   existing image from your gallery (up to **10 MB** per image).
3. Tap **Submit**—FieldLog uploads and verifies each image securely and links it
   to your observation.

### Automatic GPS capture

1. The first time you open a GPS-enabled form, allow location access when your
   browser asks for permission.
2. Watch the status pill in the bottom-right corner:
   - `Acquiring location...` — Waiting for a high-accuracy GPS fix.
   - `Location captured` *(green)* — Latitude, longitude, and accuracy (in
     meters) are locked in and will be saved with your submission.
   - `Location unavailable` — Location permission was denied or the device has
     no GPS receiver; you can still submit your observation without coordinates.

## Working offline in remote areas

Fieldwork rarely happens next to reliable Wi-Fi. When you are **signed in**,
FieldLog uses a local offline database (**PowerSync**) right inside your
browser:

1. **Open your session while you still have a signal** (at camp, the office, or
   the trailhead) so the form and local database initialize on your device.
2. **Keep submitting observations offline** — Entries save straight to your
   device's local storage with zero lag, even in airplane mode or dead zones.
3. **Automatic background sync** — As soon as your phone regains a connection,
   FieldLog uploads all queued observations to the server automatically.

:::tip Add FieldLog to your phone's home screen
FieldLog is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On iOS Safari (**Share → Add to Home
Screen**) or Android Chrome (**Menu → Install app / Add to Home screen**), you
can install FieldLog as a full-screen app icon for instant access in the field.
:::
